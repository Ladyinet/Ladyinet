# Olist — E-commerce Analytics

**Business / Product Analytics · E-commerce**

[🌐 Open live demo](https://ladyinet.fr/olist/)

## Overview
A portfolio analytics project based on the Brazilian Olist e-commerce dataset. The goal is to move beyond exploratory analysis and frame the work as a business case: understand performance, customer behaviour, seller performance and operational drivers that can support decisions.

## Portfolio focus
- business and product KPIs;
- customer and seller analysis;
- order and delivery performance;
- customer satisfaction signals;
- visual communication of findings;
- actionable recommendations.
